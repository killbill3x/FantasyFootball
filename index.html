Official League Bylaws & Special Rules

1. Draft Lottery & Scheduling Protocol

  • Lottery Timing: Draft Lottery takes place by Commissioner Billy Mehnert on the 1st Sunday of August each year.
  • Inverse Draw Mechanics: Names are drawn by hand and in inverse order of draft picks. The 1st name drawn drafts 10th, down to the last name drawn who receives the #1 overall pick.
  • Draft Date & Timer: The live draft is held the last Saturday before the start of the regular season at 7:00 PM EST. Pick timer is set to 1 minute.
  • Pick Swapping: Team managers are officially permitted to swap draft positions.

2. Rule Changes & Last Place Penalties

  • Nomination & Voting: All rule changes for the current season must be nominated during the live Lottery drawing. Managers vote on each nomination; once the voting window closes, rules are locked until the following year.
  • Last Place Name Change Penalty: The last-place team from the previous regular season is subject to a mandatory team name change voted on during the live Lottery.
  • Last Place Exemption: If the last-place team finished with more than 100 points higher than the 9th-place team, the 9th-place team is saddled with the team name change instead.

3. Scoring & Yardage Bonuses

  • Kicking: Scored by yards (0.1 points per yard on successful kicks). No penalty for missed kicks outside of 50 yards.
  • WR / RB Yardage Bonuses: +5 points if a running back or wide receiver eclipses 100 yards; an additional +5 points if they eclipse 200 yards (Max +10 bonus points).
  • Quarterback Bonuses: +5 points at 300 passing yards; an additional +5 points at 400 passing yards (Max +10 bonus points).

4. League Integrity & Etiquette

  • Draft Attendance: Team managers will be removed from the league if they fail to show up to the draft and their team is auto-selected.
  • Engagement Requirement: Team managers will be removed at the conclusion of the season if they do not engage with other league members (ensuring the right cultural fit).
  • Lineup Neglect Penalty: Any manager failing to set their lineup will be removed from the league. Commissioners Billy Mehnert and Thomas Walker will manually set their lineup for the rest of the season based on projected points.
